# Photograph and historical sources

`public/assets/einstein-1921.jpg` is a resized, JPEG-compressed copy of Ferdinand Schmutzer's 1921 photograph of Albert Einstein in Vienna.

- [Wikimedia Commons file record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Einstein_1921_by_F_Schmutzer.jpg)
- [Original JPEG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/66/Einstein_1921_by_F_Schmutzer.jpg)
- Photographer: Ferdinand Schmutzer (1870–1928)
- Source classification: public domain; see the file record's licensing section.
- Local processing: resize to 720 px wide and JPEG compression only. No image generation, reconstruction, recolouring or AI enhancement.

## Paper feature image

`public/assets/gravitation-light-1911.svg` is a 1600 × 480 code-native teaser for Albert Einstein's 1911 paper, *Über den Einfluß der Schwerkraft auf die Ausbreitung des Lichtes* (Annalen der Physik, 35, 898–908; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.19113401005)). It embeds an unchanged PNG reproduction of Figure 1; it is a modern reproduction, not a scan of the original printing.

- [Wikimedia Commons file record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Einstein(1911)_001.png)
- [Original PNG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/50/Einstein%281911%29_001.png)
- Reproduction: RWMA, 9 January 2024; CC0 1.0 Universal, as recorded by Commons.
- The 492 × 679 PNG is embedded byte-for-byte and displayed with its full aspect ratio. SVG text and a plain background provide the wide teaser layout. No image generation, retouching or scientific reconstruction.
- Academic enables this optional banner to demonstrate a paper image, caption and DOI link. Casual has no banner. Sites can omit the entire `banner` configuration.
