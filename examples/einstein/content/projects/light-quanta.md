Einstein proposed that light could transfer energy in discrete packets. His explanation of the photoelectric effect became the work specifically named in his Nobel Prize citation.

The prize was for 1921 and was awarded in 1922.

[Nobel Prize facts and historical context](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/einstein/facts/).
