Einstein's *On the Electrodynamics of Moving Bodies* appeared in 1905. It set out the theory now called special relativity, reconsidering how observers compare measurements of time and distance.

[Read the Library of Congress guide to the 1905 papers](https://guides.loc.gov/einstein-annus-mirabilis/1905-papers).
