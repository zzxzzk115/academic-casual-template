Albert Einstein was born in Ulm on 14 March 1879 and died in Princeton on 18 April 1955. His work on relativity, light quanta and statistical physics helped reshape twentieth-century physics. He studied in Zurich and later worked at the Institute for Advanced Study. Music and correspondence were lasting parts of his life. This profile presents historical material in a reusable academic and personal website layout; references and photograph credits are linked in the source notes.
