This is a modern reading note for the template example, rather than an article attributed to Einstein.

The [Library of Congress guide](https://guides.loc.gov/einstein-annus-mirabilis/1905-papers) introduces the 1905 papers on light quanta, Brownian motion, special relativity and mass–energy equivalence.

For the original publication records, use the DOI links in the [bibliography](/publications/). Titles there retain the original German. The example's research pages provide short English introductions.
