This is a historical profile used to demonstrate a reusable website template. It is not an official Einstein website. The reading notes are modern editorial material, and no personal email or social account is invented for Einstein.

## Historical references

- [Nobel Prize biography and facts](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/einstein/facts/): life dates, the photoelectric effect and the 1921 prize awarded in 1922.
- [ETH Library](https://library.ethz.ch/en/collections-and-archives/platforms/einstein-online/studium-am-polytechnikum-in-zuerich-1896-1900.html): studies from 1896 to 1900.
- [Institute for Advanced Study](https://www.ias.edu/arts-at-ias): the 1933–1955 faculty period and Einstein's interest in music.
- [Library of Congress](https://guides.loc.gov/einstein-annus-mirabilis/1905-papers): a guide to the 1905 papers.
- [Collected Papers index, Princeton University Press](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/books/einstein11/c_index.pdf): correspondence, violin playing and sailing.
